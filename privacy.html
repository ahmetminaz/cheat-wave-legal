English

Cheat Wave: Codes, Map & Guide ("the app") is an unofficial fan-made companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any game publisher or developer. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Operator and data controller: Seymanur Tuncel · Contact: servetminaz@gmail.com

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and progress (chosen console, favorite cheats, completed missions, collectible counters, save-reminder state, spoiler preference, onboarding status) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

If you subscribe to Cheat Wave Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to validate and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, device and app version information, and your IP address (used for approximate country detection). We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

How we use it

Only to verify that you have an active subscription, to unlock Pro features and to let you restore purchases.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Apple/Google (payments) and RevenueCat (subscription management), as described above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you beyond what RevenueCat processes for subscriptions, requests about subscription data can be sent to servetminaz@gmail.com. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID / Google Play subscription settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
